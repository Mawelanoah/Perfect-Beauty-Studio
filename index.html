About Perfect Beauty Studio

Perfect Beauty Studio is a beauty studio based in Moreleta Park, Pretoria, offering a range of beauty and nail services by appointment.

With a focus on the Russian Technique Manicure, our team utilises modern materials to ensure long-lasting results, while also providing high-quality services for brows and lashes.

Whether you're preparing for a special occasion or simply taking time for yourself, our goal is to provide a welcoming and professional beauty experience.
